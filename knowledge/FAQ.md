## Getting Started

**What is Soko-Link?**
Soko-Link is a multi-seller marketplace connecting buyers with
independent sellers, supported by delivery personnel and platform
administrators. You can discover products, compare sellers, place
orders, and track delivery — all in one place.

**Do I need an account to browse products?**
No, browsing and searching products doesn't require an account.
You'll need one to place an order, track it, or message a seller.

**How do I create an account?**
Sign up from the registration page with your name, email, and phone
number. You'll need to verify your account before placing orders.

**I forgot my password — what do I do?**
Use "Forgot password" on the login page to reset it via email. If you
don't receive the reset email within a few minutes, check spam, and
let support know if it still doesn't arrive.

## Products & Sellers

**How do I know a seller is trustworthy?**
Seller profiles show verification status and business information.
Look for a verified badge before ordering, and check the seller's
listed details on their profile.

**Can I contact a seller before ordering?**
Yes, buyers can message sellers directly about products or orders
through the platform.

**Why can't I find a product I'm looking for?**
Product availability depends on what sellers currently have listed.
If a search doesn't return what you need, try broader keywords or a
different category — or ask the agent to search for you.

## Orders

**How do I place an order?**
Add a product to your cart from its listing page and check out.
You'll choose a delivery address and payment method during checkout.

**Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?**
This depends on how far the order has progressed. If it hasn't been
confirmed or shipped yet, changes or cancellation are usually
possible — check the specific order's current status first, since
that determines what's still possible.

**How do I check my order status?**
Give the agent your order ID (and the email on your account) and it
will look up the live status for you — this isn't something covered
by general FAQs since it changes per order.

## Payments

**What payment methods does Soko-Link support?**
Soko-Link supports M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, bank transfer, and
cash on delivery — availability depends on what the specific seller
has enabled for that order.

**Is it safe to pay through Soko-Link?**
Payments are processed through the platform's payment system rather
than sent directly and informally to a seller. Always complete
payment through the official checkout flow, not an outside channel a
seller or "agent" asks you to use instead.

**My payment didn't go through / I was charged but the order didn't confirm — what now?**
Don't retry blindly. Give the agent the order ID and account email so
it can check the live payment status — a mobile money charge can
sometimes take a few minutes to reflect on the platform even when it
succeeded.

**How do refunds work?**
Refund eligibility and timing depend on the specific order and
dispute outcome. Ask the agent to check the payment status for that
order, and if a refund is owed but not reflected, this can be escalated
to a human agent.

## Delivery

**How long does delivery take?**
Delivery time varies by seller, delivery method, and location — there
isn't one fixed timeframe across the whole marketplace. Check the
specific order's delivery status for an estimate.

**Can I track my delivery in real time?**
Yes, once an order has been picked up, delivery status updates are
available. Ask the agent to check a specific order's delivery status.

**What if my delivery seems delayed or hasn't updated in a while?**
Ask the agent to check the live delivery status first. If it looks
stuck or something seems wrong, this can be escalated to a human
agent or the delivery/seller can be contacted through the platform.

**What if an item arrives damaged or wrong?**
This should be reported as soon as possible. It can start as a
message to the seller, and if unresolved, escalated into a dispute
for a platform administrator to review.

## Account & Support

**How do I update my account details?**
Account settings (name, phone, address, password) can be updated from
your profile/settings page.

**How do I reach a human if the agent can't help?**
Ask the agent to escalate — it can flag the conversation for a human
support agent to pick up, along with the details already discussed so
you don't have to repeat everything.

**Can Soko-Link staff see my private messages with a seller?**
Platform administrators can access transaction and dispute-related
information as needed to resolve issues, but general buyer-seller
messages aren't something the agent shares outside of an active
support or dispute case.

## Becoming a Seller

**How do I start selling on Soko-Link?**
Register a business/seller account from the sign-up page. You'll be
able to list products, manage inventory and pricing, and receive
orders once your account is set up.

**Is there a verification process for sellers?**
Yes, seller/business accounts go through a verification step before
gaining full trust indicators shown to buyers (e.g. a verified badge).
